# On-the-fly Dynamics Demonstrations

## GROMACS Demos

### Mean-Squared Displacements (MSD)

We utilize a ring buffer to compute the mean-squared displacement (MSD) of a water molecule in a simple simulation

### Velocity Auto Correlation Functions (VACF)

We utilize a custom-built C-library to compute velocity auto-correlation functions (VACF) of water molecules and compare the convergence for all water molecules in a simulations to a single molecule. 
The C-library uses shared-memory parallelization with openMP and can utilize multiple cores in parallel (avoiding limitations for parallel processing in standard python). 
We only analyze a small subset of the data computed by the library, which automatically processes translational, rotational and (if present) vibrational degrees of freedom of every residue/molecule in the selection.

## NAMD Demo

### Ion Flux

In this example, we will analyze the electric currents generated by ions that travel thorugh a protein nanopore embedded in a lipid bilayer (example provided by Chris Maffeo of the Aksimentiev group at UIUC).
While the MD simulation and analysis ran on the same computer in the two examples above, in this case the simulation will run on one of our cluster nodes and we will stream the data via a network connection.
